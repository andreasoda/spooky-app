# Spooky

**Your Mac, on your iPad.** Use your Mac from your iPad or iPhone as if it were in front of you: a sharp, smooth picture, the Mac's sound and your iPad's microphone. At home or away.

- Website: https://andreasoda.github.io/spooky-app/
- Download for Mac: [latest release](https://github.com/andreasoda/spooky-app/releases/latest)
- [How to install](https://andreasoda.github.io/spooky-app/install.html) · [Privacy policy](https://andreasoda.github.io/spooky-app/privacy.html)
- Questions and feedback: [issues](https://github.com/andreasoda/spooky-app/issues)
